Overview

Bento Project Studio 2.0 brings the Bento project editor into your DAW. Open a project from your SD card, edit its tracks, pads, zones and sequences, play your tracks from MIDI, and send each one to its own output in your DAW. It is a VST3 and Audio Unit plugin, and also a standalone application.

What's new in 2.0

  • Runs inside your DAW as a VST3 or Audio Unit plugin, and also as a standalone application.
  • A new, faster audio engine.
  • A redesigned interface that scales to the size of the window, with dark and light themes.
  • Follows your DAW tempo, and plays from MIDI: each track listens to its own channel.
  • Sends the Main mix, every track and the FX returns to separate outputs of your DAW.
  • Drag sequences, scenes and chords into your DAW as MIDI, and recordings and generated sounds as audio.
  • New Compose tools: scales, chords and Euclidean rhythms.
  • New Modulation: an LFO and other sources routed to the parameters of each track, audible thanks to the new audio engine.
  • Complete support for Granular and Wavetable tracks.
  • New Tuner.

Key features

Tracks, pads and samples

Work with Bento projects the way the hardware does: eight tracks, sixteen pads per track.

  • Open projects from your SD card or from any folder, with a file browser built into the plugin.
  • Track types: One-shot, Multisample, Slicer, Loop, Granular, Wavetable and Shredder.
  • Drag samples and folders onto pads and tracks, and set the start and end markers on the waveform.
  • Level, pan, pitch, velocity, filter, resonance, overdrive, delay and reverb sends, an envelope, and every playback mode: Trigger, Gate, Toggle, loops, poly modes and exclusive groups.
  • Play the pads from the on-screen keyboard or from MIDI.

Multisample zones

Build playable instruments visually.

  • Drop a folder of WAV files onto a Multisample track to create the zones automatically.
  • Read root notes from WAV metadata when available.
  • Edit key ranges and velocity ranges by dragging the edges of a zone, or type Root, Key Low, Key High, Vel Low and Vel High.

Sequencer and scenes

Build sequences and scenes like on the hardware, with a piano roll.

  • Eight sequence slots per track and up to sixteen scenes, launched in time with the grid while your DAW plays.
  • A Song mode plays your scenes one after another, each with its own repeat count. Loop groups launch sets of loops together.
  • Piano roll with velocity, probability and automation lanes, zoom, copy and paste, and scale lock.
  • Import MIDI files into a sequence.
  • Drag a sequence or a whole scene into your DAW as a MIDI file.

Compose

Find chords and rhythms without leaving the plugin.

  • Pick a scale, then lock new notes to it in the piano roll, or force existing notes onto the scale.
  • The Chorder turns one note into a chord, with chord type, inversion, voicing (Close, Open, Drop 2) and octave.
  • Save chords into a progression, rearrange them, and drag the whole progression out as MIDI.
  • Euclidean rhythms: set the note, steps, pulses and rotation of each line, and fill a sequence.

Modulation

Move your sounds with an LFO and other sources.

  • An LFO with nine shapes, rate, depth, Beat Sync and Restart.
  • Route sources to targets such as Level, Pitch, Pan, Attack, Decay, Release and Filter Cutoff, with up to three sources per target.
  • Granular and Wavetable tracks have two LFOs.
  • Modulation is audible, thanks to the new audio engine.

Mixer, FX and outputs

A mixer inside the plugin, and separate outputs to your DAW.

  • Eight track strips, FX returns and Main, with fader, pan, mute, solo and meters.
  • Send the Main mix, each of the eight tracks and the FX returns to separate stereo outputs of your DAW.
  • Delay and reverb as send effects, each with its own settings.
  • DJ FX: two XY pads with Repeater, Gater, Filter, Bitcrusher, Echo, Flanger and more, with Hold.
  • The Main level, the mixer and the DJ FX can be automated by your DAW.

Recorder

Capture your performance as audio.

  • Record the Main mix with or without FX, or stems: one file per track, plus FX and Main.
  • Start recording with the sequencer, and stop manually or after a fixed number of bars.
  • Drag one stem, or all stems at once, straight into your DAW tracks.

Tuner

Check the pitch of a sample or of your mix.

  • Live mode listens to Main or to the selected track. Sample mode reads the sample of the selected pad.
  • Shows the note, cents, frequency and clarity, with a spectrum by semitone.
  • Hold, A4 reference and a gate level.

Audio Editor

Prepare samples without leaving the project.

  • Normalize, Silence, Fade In and Out, Gain, Compressor, Transient Shaper, Distortion, Bit Crush, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Pitch / Time and more.
  • Loop Finder to find smoother loop points.
  • Zoom, snap to markers and zero crossings, play or loop a selection, with Undo and Redo.
  • Save WAV, Export and Trim WAV.

Sound Generator

Create new sounds and use them right away.

  • Seventeen generators: kick, snare, hat, clap, VA subtractive, physical, glitch, noise, super pad, wavetable, phase, organ, FX, FM, modal, vocal and bass.
  • Randomize to explore new results quickly.
  • Set the root note and a chord, then drag the sound onto a pad, a track, or into your DAW.

Built for your DAW

It behaves like a plugin, not like a window you have to leave.

  • Follows your DAW tempo, with Host Sync and Restart on Play.
  • Each track listens to its own MIDI channel, or the selected track plays everything. Your project is saved with your DAW session.
  • A scalable interface in four themes: Dark, Dark Soft, Light and Light Slate.

Try it first

The demo runs until you activate it with a license key.

  • The first two tracks play. Tracks 3 to 8 are disabled.
  • Saving a project with more than two tracks, and writing WAV files, are disabled.
  • Everything else works. Activating a license unlocks all eight tracks at once.
