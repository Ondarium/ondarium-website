Overview

Frametron is a video sampler plugin that turns a video into an instrument. Load a clip, cut it into slices, and play the slices from a MIDI keyboard or from your DAW's piano roll, with the sound and the picture following your notes. Add live effects to the picture, then export the video in sync with your audio bounce and drop both in your video editor or your DAW video track.

Key features

Video as an instrument

Load a video, cut it into slices and play them from your keyboard, a MIDI controller or the DAW.

  • Cut the video into equal grid divisions or at the attacks of its audio, and add or remove markers by hand.
  • Each note plays a slice with its own sound, at the speed and pitch you play it, backwards if you like.
  • Play modes One-shot, Gate and Toggle, loops Off, Forward and Ping-pong, Poly or Mono voices, and an ADSR envelope.
  • Limit a long video to the region you need.

Key zones

Decide which keys play which slice, and at which pitch.

  • Four layouts: one key per slice, the selected slice across the whole keyboard, an octave per slice, or custom zones.
  • The keyboard and the timeline share the same colours, so a slice and its keys are linked at a glance.
  • Draw zones by dragging on the keys and set a root note per zone.
  • Video follows pitch: the picture plays at the transposed speed like the sound, or keeps its own speed.

Seek mode

Turn the timeline into a seek bar and play the video by hand.

  • Click to jump to a frame and start playing; the height of the mouse sets the speed, from x-64 to x64, pause included.
  • Your gestures are recorded by the DAW as automation, exact to the frame, or as MIDI notes and controllers.
  • Use the mod wheel as a jog, a slice selector, a position or a transposition.

MIDI control and automation

Every control can be driven from a controller or automated by the DAW.

  • MIDI Learn on any knob, selector or effect bar, or edit the mapping in a dedicated window.
  • Mappings are global or saved with the project, and can be exported and imported as readable files.
  • Speed, slice, transpose, position and the effects are plugin parameters your DAW can automate.
  • Optional MIDI out, to record what you do with the mouse on a MIDI track.

Video effects

Shape the picture while it plays.

  • 25 effects in five families: Colour, Geometry, Distortion, Blur and light, Time.
  • Five effects at once, each with a start and an end value.
  • Modulate them with velocity, a note envelope, an LFO locked to the host tempo, or the audio level.
  • Echo, trail, freeze, strobe and dissolve for time-based looks.

Video export in sync with your bounce

The DAW writes the audio, Frametron writes a video file that starts at the same point.

  • Arm the Export button, run an audio mixdown in your DAW, and drop both files in your video editor or your DAW video track.
  • H.264 (all intra for editing, or compact for sharing), ProRes 422, DNxHR, FFV1 lossless, Motion JPEG and RAW.
  • The frame rate follows your project; the size is the video's or half; the plugin's own audio can be included.
  • Hardware H.264 encoding and decoding on your graphics card, if you want it.

Made for long sessions

Open the videos you have and pick up where you left off.

  • Reads most video files through FFmpeg: MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM and more, including H.264, HEVC and ProRes.
  • The images are cached in memory for instant jumps and scrubbing, and on disk so a long video reloads in seconds.
  • Everything is saved in your DAW project, and a moved video can be located again without losing your slices.
  • Dark and light themes, and a window you can resize.

Try it first

Frametron runs in demo mode until you activate it with a license key.

  • Everything works: playback, slices, Seek mode, effects, MIDI and video export.
  • A FRAMETRON DEMO watermark is shown on the preview and written in every image of the exported video.
  • Saving projects works as usual, with no time limit.
  • One key activates Frametron on up to five of your machines.

Good to know

Short videos work best

Unlike a video editor, which reads a video in one direction, Frametron has to jump to any image at any moment and play it forwards, backwards or at any speed, with no delay. To do that, it decodes the images in advance and keeps them in memory. The longer the video, the more memory it needs and the longer it takes to load, so load only the part you need: cut the video beforehand in a video editor, or narrow the Region.
